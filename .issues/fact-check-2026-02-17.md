## INCORRECT — Factual errors requiring correction

### I-1. Rakuten acquisition year (Line 138)

**Current:** `That pivot worked. We were acquired by Rakuten in 2014.`
**Correct:** Manifest Commerce was acquired by Rakuten Marketing on **February 25, 2016**.
**Sources:** Crunchbase, Marketing Dive, MediaPost, Manifest team Medium announcement.
**Fix:** Change "2014" to "2016".

### I-2. One Kings Lane / Gilt Groupe comparison (Line 110)

**Current:** `Several competitors who stayed focused on their original niches—like One Kings Lane and Gilt Groupe—achieved better outcomes with far less capital.`
**Correct:** Both were fire sales. One Kings Lane sold to Bed Bath & Beyond for ~$12-30M (from $900M peak). Gilt Groupe sold to Hudson's Bay for $250M (from ~$1.1B peak). Neither achieved "better outcomes."
**Fix:** Replace with companies that actually stayed focused and succeeded, or reframe the comparison (e.g., "competitors who stayed focused avoided the spectacular burnout" or use examples like Etsy, Wayfair).

### I-3. Notion origin story (Line 308)

**Current:** `Notion started as a tool for developers to create documentation.`
**Correct:** Notion was founded in 2013 as a **no-code tool builder/website builder for non-technical people**. Ivan Zhao wanted to let people without coding skills create their own tools.
**Fix:** Change to "Notion started as a no-code tool builder for non-technical users" or similar.

### I-4. Salesforce in Technology Pivots table (Line 5078)

**Current:** `| Salesforce | Perpetual software | Cloud CRM | $200B+ market cap |`
**Correct:** Salesforce was **cloud-native from inception** in 1999. Its famous slogan was "No Software." It never sold perpetual software.
**Fix:** Remove Salesforce from this table or replace with a company that actually made a technology pivot (e.g., Oracle moving from on-premise to cloud).

### I-5. Dropbox referral stat (Line 3039)

**Current:** `- **Result**: 60% of all signups came from referrals; signups increased 60%`
**Correct:** **35%** of daily signups came from referrals. The overall signup rate permanently **increased by 60%**. The "60%" refers to the increase, not the share from referrals.
**Fix:** Change to `- **Result**: 35% of daily signups came from referrals; overall signups increased 60%`

### I-6. Facebook narrowing funnel sequence (Lines 3767-3770)

**Current:**
```
- V1: "Social networking for everyone"
- V2: "Social networking for college students"
- V3: "Social networking for Ivy League students"
- V4: "Social networking for Harvard"

```
**Correct:** Real Facebook went **bottom-up**: Harvard (Feb 2004) → Ivy League → all colleges → high schools → everyone (Sep 2006). The sequence in the book is reversed.

```

- V2: "Social networking for Ivy League students"
- V3: "Social networking for college students"
- V4: "Social networking for everyone"
- Success at each stage → Then expand outward
```
